# Thread

Swing on a vine, let go at the right moment, fly through the gap. One tap.

## Physics
Branches sit AHEAD of your flight path, so you catch each vine from behind
and below and sweep about 95 degrees of arc. Energy is conserved during a
swing but carried from your entry. Pendulum period 3.69s, amplitude 71 deg.
